# Seven DAMA-DMBOK2 Environmental Elements

## Source and interpretation

[DAMA-DMBOK2 Framework, section 4.1.3, pages 13–15](https://itmatters.com.au/resources/DAMA-DMBOK2-Framework-V2-20140317-FINAL.pdf)
identifies seven Environmental Elements for describing each data management
knowledge area. Its page 15 table provides the exact English names below and
relates them to context diagrams and activity groups. These are management
elements, not data quality dimensions. The assessment questions and evidence
examples below are this skill's operational guidance, not quotations or
official DAMA scoring criteria.

Assess every element. For each, record the question asked, observed evidence,
finding, gap, recommended action, and unresolved facts. If evidence is missing,
report `Not assessed` with the missing source or owner; do not infer maturity.

## 1. Goals & Principles

- **Framework connection:** Goals in the context diagram and planning activities.
- **Assessment question:** Are the purposes, desired outcomes, and governing
  principles for the data documented and understood by its owners and users?
- **Possible evidence:** Approved objectives, data policies, business rules,
  agreed quality targets, and owner confirmation.

## 2. Activities

- **Framework connection:** Processes and their activity indicators or
  classifications.
- **Assessment question:** Which acquisition, validation, maintenance, control,
  and delivery activities actually occur, and at what cadence?
- **Possible evidence:** Run histories, workflow descriptions, schedules,
  exception queues, and observed operating procedures.

## 3. Deliverables

- **Framework connection:** Inputs and deliverables across activities.
- **Assessment question:** What inputs are required, what outputs are produced,
  and can users trace their purpose and status?
- **Possible evidence:** Source inventories, dataset specifications, quality
  reports, lineage records, and named output consumers.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Framework connection:** Supplier, responsible, consumer, and stakeholder
  roles across activities.
- **Assessment question:** Who supplies, produces, approves, maintains, and
  consumes the data, and who decides how issues are resolved?
- **Possible evidence:** Ownership records, responsibility assignments,
  approvals, escalation paths, and interviews with those roles.

## 5. Practices & Techniques

- **Framework connection:** Techniques and metrics across activities.
- **Assessment question:** Which repeatable methods and measures are used to
  profile, validate, transform, monitor, and improve the data?
- **Possible evidence:** Rule catalogs, profiling methods, test specifications,
  measurement definitions, and documented exception handling.

## 6. Tools

- **Framework connection:** Toolsets that may support activities.
- **Assessment question:** Which actual tools support the work, and can their
  use and outputs be verified?
- **Possible evidence:** System inventories, pipeline configuration, catalog
  entries, monitoring outputs, and access-controlled demonstrations.

## 7. Organization & Culture

- **Framework connection:** An element relevant across activities.
- **Assessment question:** Do teams share ownership of data issues, communicate
  changes, and act on findings in routine work?
- **Possible evidence:** Governance meeting records, issue resolution history,
  change communication, training materials, and stakeholder interviews.
